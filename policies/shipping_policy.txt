OMNIDESK GLOBAL SHIPPING & LOGISTICS POLICY (2026 EDITION)

1. DOMESTIC SHIPPING TIERS & DELIVERY TIMELINES
OmniDesk offers three domestic shipping tiers across all serviceable pin codes in India:
- Standard Ground Shipping: Takes 4 to 6 business days. Free on all orders above Rs. 999. For orders below Rs. 999, a flat fee of Rs. 79 applies.
- Express Air Shipping: Takes 1 to 2 business days. Costs a flat fee of Rs. 249 regardless of order value. Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM IST for same-day dispatch from our Hyderabad or Bengaluru fulfillment centers.
- Same-Day Hyperlocal Delivery: Available exclusively in Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Bengaluru, and Mumbai for orders placed before 11:00 AM IST. Costs Rs. 399.

2. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING & CUSTOMS DUTIES
International orders are shipped via DHL Express and take 5 to 8 business days. International shipping rates are calculated dynamically based on package weight, starting at Rs. 1,800 for the first 500 grams.
Important Customs Policy: Import duties, VAT, and local customs clearance taxes are NOT included in the item price or shipping cost. The customer is 100% responsible for paying customs duties upon arrival in the destination country.

3. LOST, DELAYED, OR DAMAGED PACKAGES IN TRANSIT
If a package tracking status has not updated for more than 7 consecutive days (domestic) or 12 consecutive days (international), the shipment is officially classified as "Lost in Transit." Customers are eligible for a free replacement shipment or a 100% full refund.
If an Express Air shipment arrives later than the 2-business-day SLA due to carrier delays, the customer is entitled to a full refund of the Rs. 249 shipping fee, though the product itself cannot be refunded solely due to a 1-day carrier delay.

4. ADDRESS CHANGES & ORDER REROUTING
Customers may change their delivery address free of charge while the order status is "Processing" or "Packed." Once the package has been handed over to the courier and the status changes to "In Transit" or "Out for Delivery," address modifications incur a Rs. 150 carrier rerouting fee.
